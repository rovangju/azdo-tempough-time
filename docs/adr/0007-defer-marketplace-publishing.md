# 7. Defer Marketplace publishing

Date: 2026-08-20

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The initial goal is local development and private packaging. Publisher identity and Marketplace material are not ready.

## Consequences

Publishing remains a separate handoff. Replace the mock publisher and add noninteractive publish/update automation later.
